### 4. Status nuance for Minenergo 2019 KII/TЭK recommendations

Habr lists both the 2019 Minenergo/FSTEC methodical recommendations and Minenergo letter 28.02.2024 No. 15-203.

Letter 15-203 explicitly states that the methodical recommendations:

- contain no normative-law prescriptions and are recommendatory;
- may be used only insofar as they do not contradict Russian legislation;
- were planned for later updating in view of changes in KII legislation.

Because major KII reforms followed in 2025-2026, the safe graph/status is **not** `CURRENT_NORMATIVE_REQUIREMENT` and **not** `REPEALED`.

Classification: `NON_NORMATIVE_GUIDANCE / COMPATIBILITY_CHECK_REQUIRED / POST_REFORM_STALENESS_RISK`.

This is a status-quality gate for KB import: recommendation documents should not inherit the status semantics of registered NPA merely because they appear next to them in Habr.
