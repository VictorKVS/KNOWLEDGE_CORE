## 2. Technical delta: DDoS / denial-of-service protection for significant CII objects

Reliable full-text legal copies of No. 159 confirm that its item 2 amends FSTEC No. 239/2017 and introduces a distinct protection measure family for attacks aimed at denial of service. Among the explicit requirements are:
- inventory of Internet-facing interfaces/services, public network addresses and domain names;
- removal/closure of unused or unattributed Internet-facing interfaces/services/addresses/domains;
- a communication matrix for interaction between significant CII objects and the Internet;
- a list of network addresses that must remain allowed during an attack;
- network/application-layer traffic analysis, filtering and blocking;
- a twofold reserve of data-channel bandwidth relative to normal non-attack traffic;
- use of data received through interaction with the Center for Monitoring and Management of the Public Communications Network;
- retention for three years of specified facts/parameters concerning denial-of-service attacks.

KB edge: `FSTEC_159_2024 --AMENDS--> FSTEC_239_2017`.

## 3. New lifecycle nuance: Order No. 159 is only partially inoperative from 01.03.2026

FSTEC No. 117 of 11.04.2025 was officially published 17.06.2025 (publication ID `0001202506170011`, Minjust No. 82619) and entered into force on 01.03.2026.

No. 117 expressly recognizes as invalid:
- FSTEC No. 17/2013; and
- **item 1** of the amendments approved by FSTEC No. 159/2024, i.e. the part that amended No. 17.

It does **not** invalidate item 2 of No. 159, which amends FSTEC No. 239/2017. Current full-text presentation of No. 159 therefore shows item 1 as invalid from 01.03.2026 while item 2 remains operative.

Do not normalize the whole amending act as `REPEALED`.

Store section-level lifecycle:
- `FSTEC_159_2024.item_1 --AMENDS--> FSTEC_17_2013`, operative until 28.02.2026;
- `FSTEC_117_2025 --REPEALS--> FSTEC_17_2013`, effective 01.03.2026;
- `FSTEC_117_2025 --MAKES_INOPERATIVE--> FSTEC_159_2024.item_1`, effective 01.03.2026;
- `FSTEC_159_2024.item_2 --AMENDS--> FSTEC_239_2017`, remains relevant/current.

Recommended aggregate node state: `PARTIALLY_INOPERATIVE`, not `REPEALED`.
