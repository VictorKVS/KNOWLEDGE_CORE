The current secondary full text of № 214 (edition 21.08.2025) says that item numbers correspond to the list approved by Government Resolution **1 July 1996 № 770**.

Also, Government Resolution 21.08.2025 № 1250 amended № 214; secondary current sources show effect from 30.08.2025. This supports the conclusion that the № 770 list remains functionally referenced after the 2025 update to № 214.
