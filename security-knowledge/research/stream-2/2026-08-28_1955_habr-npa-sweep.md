## Version/status rule added

For ПНСТ, a boolean `CURRENT` is insufficient. Persist and evaluate at least:

- `approved_at`
- `effective_from`
- `valid_until` / `expires_on`
- `status_as_of`
- `replaces`
- `replaced_by`
- `official_source`

Do not infer supersession when the official Rosstandart card shows no replacing/replaced designation. As of 2026-08-28 all five standards above are active, but ПНСТ 905-2023 is time-bounded through 2026-11-01 and therefore must be re-evaluated after that date.
