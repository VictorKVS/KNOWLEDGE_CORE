## New rules / blockers from this pass

1. Restricted-content boundary must be represented explicitly: an open ministerial order body does not imply that a DСП appendix/recommendations are full-text available.
2. `GitHub search = 0` is recorded as a full-text gap, not as proof that no copy exists anywhere.
3. Direct ministry PDF URLs are identity/acquisition candidates, but `OFFICIAL_BYTES_VERIFIED` is forbidden until the bytes are actually retrieved and hashed.
4. Secondary full-text copies may enrich identity fields (approver, coordination number/date) but cannot upgrade an artifact to official-primary status.
