## New confirmed deltas

### 1. PP RF 1915 of 26.12.2024 — omitted amendment node for PP RF 1912/2023
- Habr 432466 contains PP RF of 14.11.2023 No. 1912 on transition of KII subjects to preferential use of trusted software-hardware complexes (DPAK), but search of the Habr text returns no occurrence of `1915`.
- Amendment identity: Постановление Правительства РФ от 26.12.2024 № 1915 «О внесении изменений в постановление Правительства Российской Федерации от 14 ноября 2023 г. № 1912».
- Primary issuer verification: the current Government of Russia page for PP 1912 explicitly marks the text as being in the edition of PP 1915 dated 26.12.2024 and shows the amended wording.
- Substance: responsibility for organizing DPAK transition for KII subjects created by the Russian Federation for state pension provision, compulsory pension insurance and compulsory social insurance is separated from the Ministry of Finance branch and assigned to the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection; PP 1915 also required the Ministry of Labour to appoint the responsible official and approve the relevant transition plan by 31.12.2024.
- Official-publication metadata independently identifies publication id `0001202412270085`, publication date 27.12.2024, 3 pages, 446626 bytes; PP 1915 itself states that it enters into force on the date of official publication.
- Relationship: `PP_RF_1915_2024 --AMENDS--> PP_RF_1912_2023`.
- Current treatment: PP 1912 remains the base act; current edition is at least `red. 26.12.2024` created by PP 1915. Do not model 1915 as a replacement.
- Habr classification: `PRE_CUTOFF / HABR_VERSION_GRAPH_GAP` because the amendment was effective from 27.12.2024, long before Habr version 28.05.2026.
