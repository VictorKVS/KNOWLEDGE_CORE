## New conflict: incorrect date in the text of FSTEC order 69/2023

The text of FSTEC order 69/2023 cites `Указ Президента Российской Федерации от 11 мая 2022 г. № 250`.

The primary official Presidential publication proves that decree No. 250 is actually dated **01.05.2022**, title `О дополнительных мерах по обеспечению информационной безопасности Российской Федерации`, official publication ID `0001202205010023`, published 01.05.2022. The later Presidential decree 500/2024 also identifies the base act as `Указ Президента Российской Федерации от 1 мая 2022 г. № 250`.

Therefore the discrepancy is recorded as:

`DOCUMENT_INTERNAL_CITATION_DATE_TYPO / NUMBER_TITLE_IDENTITY_MATCHES / DO_NOT_CREATE_SECOND_DECREE_NODE`.

This must not generate a fake `11.05.2022 No. 250` node in the knowledge graph. Normalize the citation target to `PRESIDENT_DECREE_250_2022_05_01` while preserving the verbatim erroneous source citation as provenance.
