## Vision

FATHER Analytical Design Platform is an analytical engineering environment that turns software/system design into a visible production line of roles, evidence, canonical artifacts, reviews and gates.

A user should be able to move from a high-level lifecycle map into a specific stage, then into the exact operation, artifact, source requirement, evidence and decision that explains why the project can or cannot move forward.

## Primary capabilities

1. Visualize the entire engineering lifecycle and drill down from L0 to L3/L4.
2. Show roles, authority boundaries, inputs, operations, outputs, reviews and gates.
3. Map standards and source evidence to artifact fields and decisions.
4. Ingest existing project documents and compare them with the canonical required knowledge model.
5. Reuse existing evidence before generating new documents.
6. Keep FACT, CLAIM, ASSUMPTION, HYPOTHESIS, CONFLICT and UNKNOWN distinct.
7. Provide readiness, gap, conflict, provenance and maturity analytics.
8. Support professional agents while preventing self-certification and silent authority escalation.
9. Preserve a machine-readable evidence graph behind every material project decision.
10. Support greenfield and brownfield projects without forcing one delivery methodology.

## Product principles

- Security enters immediately after Product and remains continuous.
- System model and Threat Model v0 exist before architecture decision.
- Evidence quality is more important than file count.
- Visual representation never replaces canonical registries/evidence.
- Unknown is a valid state.
- Local projects may strengthen FATHER invariants but may not weaken them.

## Non-goals for the current factory MVP

- autonomous business-owner decisions;
- automatic legal/compliance certification;
- autonomous residual-risk acceptance;
- unrestricted multi-agent swarm;
- architecture selection before required gates;
- replacing original source material with model summaries.

## Product horizon

Current horizon: build and validate the document/evidence factory on FATHER itself, then test it on real software projects and brownfield document sets.
