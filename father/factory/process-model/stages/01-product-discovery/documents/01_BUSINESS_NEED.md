# BUSINESS NEED — FATHER Analytical Design Platform

Status: `OWNER_CONFIRMED`

Accountable owner: `business_owner`

Review: `product_manager`

Normative crosswalk: `PRODUCT_GOST_CROSSWALK.yaml`

## Problem / opportunity

Software and system design produces many interdependent decisions, artifacts, standards, regulatory constraints, reviews and evidence. In ordinary projects this knowledge is fragmented across documents, repositories, discussions, diagrams and individual specialists.

The product is needed to transform that fragmented work into a visible, evidence-backed engineering production process in which a material decision can be traced to its inputs, sources, owner, review and downstream consequences.

## Observed problem signals

- missing or contradictory inputs can silently become architecture assumptions;
- documents often exist without a visible dependency graph or readiness state;
- security and compliance may be attached too late, after architecture choices are already expensive to change;
- LLM/agent output can look authoritative without being professionally verified;
- existing project materials are often duplicated instead of mapped and reused;
- engineering reviews are difficult to reconstruct after the fact;
- users need strong visual navigation through complex engineering work.

## Required outcome

Create an analytical design environment that guides a project from Product through Security, System Engineering, Requirements, Architecture, implementation evidence and operation while preserving provenance, uncertainty, review state and accountable authority.

## Must not become

- a document generator without evidence logic;
- an architecture generator that bypasses Product/Security/System gates;
- a self-verifying agent system;
- a compliance declaration engine without applicability evidence;
- a replacement for accountable human owners or risk acceptance authority.

## Evidence state

The need and its principal problem signals are owner-confirmed for the FATHER pilot. Quantitative external market validation is not yet claimed.
